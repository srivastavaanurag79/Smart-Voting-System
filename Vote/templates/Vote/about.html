About

A prototype for trustworthy online voting

SmartVote explores how encrypted, anonymous ballots and a publicly verifiable ledger can bring transparency to elections — from home, from any device.

What it does

Encrypted ballots. Sealed to the election public key; only a tally ceremony can open them.

Anonymous by design. No stored link between a voter and their choice.

Aadhaar-anchored. OTP + biometric matched inside UIDAI; biometrics never stored here.

Verifiable. A hash-chained ledger and per-voter receipts anyone can check.
