Why it can be trusted

Security you can inspect, not just believe

◆

Encrypted ballots

Each ballot is sealed with the election's public key (RSA-OAEP + AES-256-GCM). Only a tally ceremony can open it.

⛓

Tamper-evident ledger

Ballots are appended to a hash chain. Change one and every later link breaks — visibly.

✓

Your receipt

Get a receipt proving your ballot is in the count — revealing nothing about your choice.

◈

Aadhaar-anchored

Identity is verified with Aadhaar OTP + biometrics matched inside UIDAI. Biometrics are never stored here.

☰

One person, one vote

A unique verified identity plus single-use tokens make stuffing and double voting detectable and blocked.

◎

Privately by design

Voter IDs (EPIC) and identity data are encrypted at rest; ballots carry no owner. No one can backtrack a vote.

How it works

Four steps, from anywhere

Sign in

Password, plus an optional face match for convenience.

Verify identity

Aadhaar OTP + a biometric (face, fingerprint or iris) matched 1:1 by UIDAI.

Cast your encrypted ballot

Choose one candidate per contest. Your ballot is sealed and appended anonymously.

Verify your receipt

Confirm inclusion on the public ledger at any time, without revealing your vote.
